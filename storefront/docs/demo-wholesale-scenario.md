# Wholesale & Boutique Buyer Demo Walkthrough Script (Prompt 092)

Use this walkthrough to demonstrate how **Boutique Owners, Resellers, and International Trade Buyers** use the storefront to prepare structured multi-design bulk inquiries.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough
1. **Direct B2B Entry (`/wholesale`)**:
   - Navigate to **Wholesale** from the main header. Point out the masthead affirming `Manufacturer & Wholesaler • NIT Faridabad • Worldwide Shipping`.
2. **Bulk Inquiry Configurator**:
   - Enter a sample boutique profile:
     - **Boutique / Business Name**: `Meher Luxe Boutique`
     - **Buyer Category**: `Boutique / Multi-brand Retailer`
     - **Destination City & Country**: `Leicester, UK` (or `Ludhiana, India`)
     - **Approximate Quantity Interest**: `15–30 sets / assorted`
   - Emphasize that the configurator never fabricates unverified MOQ numbers or fake wholesale discount tiers—it captures the buyer's exact lot interest for direct showroom confirmation.
3. **Selecting Designs from the Trade Gallery**:
   - Scroll to **2. Browse Designs for Bulk Enquiry** on `/wholesale`.
   - Click **Add to Wholesale Enquiry** on 3–4 designs (e.g., `ACH-DfC6hNDTwrJ`, `ACH-DGTafF6z2H4`, `ACH-DGIl9n_zE0O`).
   - Show how the selected design chips appear immediately in the configurator and sync automatically with the global **Enquiry Bag**.
4. **Live WhatsApp Trade Sheet Handoff**:
   - Inspect the **Live WhatsApp Trade Draft** panel on the right. It formats the boutique name, buyer category, destination city/country, approximate lot quantity, and numbered list of `ACH-` codes + Instagram shortcodes.
   - Click **Copy Formatted Trade Inquiry Text** or **Send Wholesale Inquiry on WhatsApp** (`+91 8810530298`).
