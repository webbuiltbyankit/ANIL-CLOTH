## 1. Diagnosis of Supplied Visual Assets

We inspected `image/profile_picture.jpg`, `image/C_pknaLv-Hc.jpg`, 36+ representative `image/<shortcode>_cover.jpg` files, and the 97 corresponding `video/<shortcode>_video.mp4` Reel videos in `anil_cloth_house/`.

### Key Visual Characteristics:
1. **Reel Cover Portraits (`97` files, `640×1136` px, `40:71` / `~9:16` ratio)**:
   - Every garment photo is a first-frame cover from an Instagram Reel filmed inside the physical showroom at NIT Faridabad.
   - **Composition**: The presenter holds a wooden hanger displaying an ornate women's occasionwear garment (heavily embroidered designer blouse paired with a lehenga skirt, pre-draped saree, or cape sleeves) at chest-to-waist height against showroom racks and green turf flooring.
   - **On-Image Text Overlays**: Most covers feature tilted blue-or-pink outlined stickers in the lower-middle zone reading `Wholesale rate ₹[amount] / [N] colours shown / WhatsApp or visit 8810530298`.
2. **Real Instagram Reel Videos (`97` `.mp4` files in `anil_cloth_house/video/`)**:
   - Each of the 97 Reel covers has its matching local `.mp4` video showing the garment in motion, including close-up pans of beadwork, back-neck tie details, colour options, and skirt/saree drape movement.
3. **Feed Notice Image (`C_pknaLv-Hc.jpg`, `1025×1025` px, `1:1` ratio)**:
   - A blue gradient text card stating `#To order / Watsapp on: +91-8810530298 / #No DM's# No COD / We do not hold items`.
   - **Rule**: Exclude from wearable fashion product cards; surface only in ordering-policy/provenance explanations.
4. **Profile Picture (`profile_picture.jpg`, `100×100` px)**:
   - Low-resolution scan of the store's pink/cream visiting card (`Adinya By Anil Cloth House`).
   - **Rule**: Do not upscale as a blurry header logo; use a refined editorial serif wordmark (`ANIL CLOTH HOUSE`) in the global header and display `profile_picture.jpg` at its native compact size in store provenance / contact cards.

## 2. Aspect Ratio, Framing & Crop Rules

- **Primary Product Card Ratio (`9:16` / `40:71` Portrait or `4:5` Contained Frame)**:
  - Never stretch `640×1136` images horizontally or crop aggressively into `16:9` landscapes, which would decapitate the garment or cut through the bodice embroidery.
  - In product grids, use a vertical portrait frame (`aspect-[9/16]` or `aspect-[3/4]` with `object-cover object-center` carefully tuned or `object-contain` on a warm stone/ivory matte backdrop `#F4EFEA`) so both the embroidered blouse and lower drape/lehenga remain legible.
- **Hero & Editorial Split Layouts**:
  - Instead of stretching a single `640×1136` cover across a `1440px` full-bleed background, use **art-directed split/composite layouts**: editorial typography on a deep oxblood (`#2C1219`) or warm ivory (`#FAF6F0`) panel alongside a crisp portrait frame showcasing the garment and optional muted Reel video playback.
- **Handling Overlaid Text Stickers**:
  - Do **not** place white/cream UI text directly over the lower-center of Reel covers where the blue/pink `Wholesale rate` sticker sits. Keep card titles, design IDs, and CTAs cleanly below the image frame or in a dedicated bottom bar.
